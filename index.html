The shift

What got cheap, and what didn’t.

I pay cents per run for

  • Research and competitor tracking
  • First drafts, threads and briefs
  • Campaign setup and reporting
  • Data cleanup and attribution

Still expensive

  • Deciding what’s true about your market
  • Choosing what you’ll say
  • Choosing what you’ll never claim
  • Being in the room with partners

You stop doing the marketing and start directing the systems that do it. Then you check their work.

How autonomy is earned

You don’t hire an agent straight into full autonomy.

Rung 1

Shadow

It runs and proposes. Nothing applies.

Rung 2 · most of mine

Approval

It proposes. A person says yes every time.

Rung 3

Guarded auto

It acts inside a fence I drew, with caps.

Rung 4

Trusted

Rare. None of mine are here yet.

What none of them will ever do

Hold a key or sign a transaction · mention token price or yield · post to an official account without approval · spend past a measured cap

Failures I already paid for

My agents never failed loudly.

What happenedThe rule it left behind
A webhook listened for the wrong event. 513 clicks lost, no errors.Every integration proves it received something recently.
A pipeline froze for nine days across 214 failed runs.A quiet system is a broken one until it proves otherwise.
A funded three-week ad flight showed as active and served nothing.Check what actually served. The status column lies.
